Phase 2 Overview

Phase 2 — Geometric Regularization for Concept-Aligned Hidden States

The Setting

We start from a paraphrase corpus of 50 concepts with 5 paraphrases per concept, totalling 250 sentences. Each sentence expresses the same concept in different surface forms:

Concept 0:
  - "the cat sat on the mat"
  - "a feline was resting on the rug"
  - "the kitten lounged on the carpet"
  - ...

Concept 1:
  - "she wrote a letter to her friend"
  - "the woman composed a note for her pal"
  - ...

We extract hidden states from a language model, then compute:

  • intra-cluster cosine — average \(\cos(h_{c,i}, h_{c,j})\) over paraphrase pairs of the same concept
  • inter-cluster cosine — average \(\cos(h_{c,i}, h_{c',j})\) over paraphrase pairs of different concepts
  • margin = intra − inter (a larger margin ⇒ better concept clustering)

The Loss

We add a cross-sentence InfoNCE loss computed on a micro-batch of \(M\) sentences drawn from \(K\) concepts:

\[ \mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{i=1}^{M} -\log \frac{\sum_{j \in \text{pos}(i)} \exp(\text{sim}(z_i, z_j)/\tau)}{\sum_{k \neq i} \exp(\text{sim}(z_i, z_k)/\tau)} \]

where \(\text{pos}(i)\) are the indices of sentences sharing the same concept as sentence \(i\), and \(z_i\) is either the last-token or mean-pool hidden state of the final non-pad position.

We pair this with a margin-push auxiliary that explicitly penalises inter-cluster cosines above 0.5:

\[ \mathcal{L}_{\text{margin}} = \text{ReLU}(\cos_{\text{neg}} - 0.5) \]

Total loss:

\[ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{LM}} + \lambda \cdot (\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} + 0.1 \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{margin}}) \]

Why this design (vs failed v1 / v2)

  • v1 (block-internal) computed InfoNCE over positions within the same sentence’s hidden states. This optimised local token-level smoothness, not cross-sentence concept alignment. Result: H-4 FAIL.
  • v2 (micro-batch only) broke the LM training distribution by replacing LM batches with paraphrase-only micro-batches. LM loss exploded to 6.85, InfoNCE = NaN. Result: H-4 FAIL.
  • v3 (LM stream + periodic micro-batch) keeps the standard LM training distribution (stream block of 128 tokens), and every \(G\) steps pauses to do one micro-batch of \(M\) paraphrases for \(\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}}\). The LM flow stays stable; geometric loss is auxiliary. Result: H-4 PASS on 0.5B.

H-4 Decision Boundary

Stage Result Δ (margin)
0.5B v1 FAIL +0.003
0.5B v2 FAIL NaN
0.5B v3 PASS +0.131
7B v5 FAIL +0.034
7B v6 FAIL +0.037

# Phase 2 — Geometric Regularization for Concept-Aligned Hidden States

## The Setting

We start from a paraphrase corpus of **50 concepts** with **5 paraphrases per concept**, totalling **250 sentences**. Each sentence expresses the same concept in different surface forms:

```
Concept 0:
  - "the cat sat on the mat"
  - "a feline was resting on the rug"
  - "the kitten lounged on the carpet"
  - ...

Concept 1:
  - "she wrote a letter to her friend"
  - "the woman composed a note for her pal"
  - ...
```

We extract hidden states from a language model, then compute:

- **intra-cluster cosine** — average $\cos(h_{c,i}, h_{c,j})$ over paraphrase pairs of the same concept
- **inter-cluster cosine** — average $\cos(h_{c,i}, h_{c',j})$ over paraphrase pairs of *different* concepts
- **margin** = intra − inter (a larger margin ⇒ better concept clustering)

## The Loss

We add a **cross-sentence InfoNCE** loss computed on a micro-batch of $M$ sentences drawn from $K$ concepts:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{i=1}^{M} -\log \frac{\sum_{j \in \text{pos}(i)} \exp(\text{sim}(z_i, z_j)/\tau)}{\sum_{k \neq i} \exp(\text{sim}(z_i, z_k)/\tau)}
$$

where $\text{pos}(i)$ are the indices of sentences sharing the same concept as sentence $i$, and $z_i$ is either the last-token or mean-pool hidden state of the *final non-pad position*.

We pair this with a **margin-push auxiliary** that explicitly penalises inter-cluster cosines above 0.5:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{margin}} = \text{ReLU}(\cos_{\text{neg}} - 0.5)
$$

Total loss:

$$
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{LM}} + \lambda \cdot (\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} + 0.1 \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{margin}})
$$

## Why this design (vs failed v1 / v2)

- **v1 (block-internal)** computed InfoNCE over *positions within the same sentence's hidden states*. This optimised local token-level smoothness, not cross-sentence concept alignment. Result: H-4 FAIL.
- **v2 (micro-batch only)** broke the LM training distribution by replacing LM batches with paraphrase-only micro-batches. LM loss exploded to 6.85, InfoNCE = NaN. Result: H-4 FAIL.
- **v3 (LM stream + periodic micro-batch)** keeps the standard LM training distribution (stream block of 128 tokens), and every $G$ steps pauses to do one micro-batch of $M$ paraphrases for $\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}}$. The LM flow stays stable; geometric loss is auxiliary. Result: H-4 **PASS** on 0.5B.
