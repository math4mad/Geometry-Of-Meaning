# 7B v6: λ upper-bound expansion

v5 left the door open — λ=1.0 was still rising monotonically. v6 expands the scan to {0, 1, 2, 5, 10} to find the saturation point.

## Identical architecture to v5

Same: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct + QLoRA r=16 α=32 4bit, T4 16GB, 2 epochs, LR=5e-5 cosine + warmup 50, BLOCK=128, MICRO_BATCH=4, GEO_EVERY=4, InfoNCE τ=0.07.

**Only change**: `LAMBDAS = [0, 1, 2, 5, 10]` (vs v5's `[0, 0.1, 0.5, 1.0]`).

**Also**: v6 fixed the v5 bug where `fl("done", ...)` overwrote the complete `out` dict containing all per-arm data. The full results now save to `metrics_georeg_7b_v6_full.json`.

## Results

| Pool | baseline | λ=0 | λ=1 | λ=2 | λ=5 | λ=10 | best Δ | H-4' |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **last** | 0.049 | 0.061 | 0.084 | 0.086 | **0.086** | 0.085 | **+0.037** (λ=5) | FAIL |
| **mean** | 0.070 | 0.072 | 0.078 | 0.082 | 0.084 | **0.085** | **+0.015** (λ=10) | FAIL |

## Saturation analysis

| λ | last margin | Δ last | mean margin | Δ mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.061 | +0.012 | 0.072 | +0.002 |
| 1 | 0.084 | +0.034 | 0.078 | +0.009 |
| 2 | 0.086 | +0.036 | 0.082 | +0.012 |
| 5 | **0.086** | **+0.037** | 0.084 | +0.014 |
| 10 | 0.085 | +0.036 | **0.085** | **+0.015** |

- **last pool saturates at λ=2-5** — 0.0855 → 0.0861 → 0.0846 (no further gain)
- **mean pool is still rising at λ=10** — 0.0784 → 0.0819 → 0.0838 → 0.0847

## v5 → v6 delta

| Pool | v5 best (λ=1) | v6 best (λ=5) | Δ improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| last | +0.034 | +0.037 | **+0.003** |
| mean | +0.009 | +0.015 | **+0.006** |

v6 only added 0.003-0.006 over v5 — **expanding λ no longer pays off**.

## Cross-scale final

| | 0.5B v3 best | 7B v6 best | 比例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Δ last | +0.131 | +0.037 | 0.28× |
| Δ mean | +0.061 | +0.015 | 0.25× |

**Geometric regularization at 7B is ~25-28% as effective as at 0.5B** (in absolute Δ).

## H-4' Verdict

**FAIL** — 7B geometric regularization max Δ is +0.037 (last, λ=5), below the +0.05 H-4 threshold. The trend is real and monotonic, but the *magnitude* in 7B is too small to claim H-4 PASS at this scale.
